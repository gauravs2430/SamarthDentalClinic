Kids Dentistry

Gentle check-ups, fluoride, sealants and milk-tooth treatment — with first visits kept short so children leave without any fear.

What it is

A child's first few dental visits decide how they feel about dentists for the rest of their life. We keep the first one deliberately short and undramatic — a look, a count, a ride in the chair — so that nothing about the place is frightening before any treatment is ever needed.

Milk teeth matter more than people assume. They hold the space for the adult teeth coming behind them, and a decayed milk tooth is painful and can damage the permanent tooth still forming underneath. Treating them properly is not optional just because they will fall out eventually.

Signs you may need this treatment

  • Your child has never been to a dentist
  • Brown or white spots on the teeth
  • Complaints of toothache or sensitivity
  • A tooth chipped or knocked in a fall
  • Thumb-sucking beyond the toddler years
  • Adult teeth coming in crooked or crowded

  • A woman dentist many children warm to quickly

The procedure

How kids dentistry is done here

Getting comfortable

The first appointment is about familiarity — meeting us, sitting in the chair, and a gentle look around.

Check-up

Teeth, gums and bite are examined, and we point out any spots that need watching.

Prevention

Fluoride application strengthens the enamel and sealants close the deep grooves in back teeth where decay starts.

Treatment if needed

Fillings, milk-tooth pulp treatment or extraction, explained to your child in words they understand.
