About the Clinic

Samarth Dental Clinic is a dental clinic in Vavol, Gandhinagar, run by Dr. Dhvani Joshi. We treat the whole family — from a child’s first check-up to implants, root canal, braces and dentures.

The clinic is at Shop No. S09, ShantiNagar Shopping, near Hotel Leela. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

  • Appointments by phone or WhatsApp
  • Digital X-rays in the clinic
  • Written treatment cost before we start
  • UPI, cards and cash accepted

Why Patients Visit Us

Clear explanation

We explain the problem, the treatment and the cost before any work starts. X-rays are shown to you and questions are welcome.

Cost in writing

You receive a written plan with the cost of each step. Where there is more than one option, we tell you the difference.

Sterilisation

Instruments are autoclaved between patients. Needles, gloves and suction tips are single-use.

Clinic Facilities

Digital X-rays

Lower radiation than film, and the image is on screen instantly so you can see what we are seeing.

Written treatment plans

Every stage costed in writing before work starts, so nothing on the final bill is a surprise.

Autoclave sterilisation

A full sterilisation cycle between patients, with single-use disposables for everything else.

UPI, cards & NFC

Pay however is easiest — contactless mobile payments, cards, UPI or cash.
