Wisdom Tooth Removal

Planned surgical removal of impacted or painful wisdom teeth, with clear aftercare so healing is straightforward.

What it is

Wisdom teeth arrive last and often find there is no room left. A tooth that comes through at an angle, or only halfway, traps food and bacteria against the molar in front of it — which is how a wisdom tooth ends up causing decay in a perfectly good neighbour, along with recurring swelling and jaw pain.

Not every wisdom tooth has to go. If yours is through, clean and biting properly, we will leave it alone and keep an eye on it. When one does need removing, an X-ray tells us the root shape and position beforehand, which is what makes the appointment predictable rather than an ordeal.

Signs you may need this treatment

  • Pain or pressure at the back of the jaw
  • Swollen, tender gum over a partly erupted tooth
  • Repeated infections in the same area
  • Difficulty opening the mouth fully
  • Decay in the wisdom tooth or the molar in front
  • A bad taste or odour from behind the last molar

The procedure

How wisdom tooth removal is done here

X-ray assessment

The angle, root shape and nerve position are checked on a digital X-ray before anything is planned.

Anaesthesia

The area is thoroughly numbed and we confirm it before starting.

Removal

The tooth is removed — sectioned into pieces if that makes it gentler on the surrounding bone — and the site is cleaned.

Aftercare

Sutures are placed if needed and you leave with written instructions, medication and a review appointment.
