Our Dental Services

12 treatments carried out under one roof in Vavol, Gandhinagar.

What we treat

Everything from a six-monthly cleaning to a full implant case

Because it all happens here, you are not sent elsewhere halfway through treatment — and the dentist who diagnosed the problem is the one who fixes it.

01

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed in the jaw to carry a single crown, a bridge or a full arch — the closest thing there is to getting your own tooth back.

Learn more
02

Root Canal Treatment

The treatment that saves a badly decayed or infected tooth instead of removing it — usually finished in one or two comfortable sittings.

Learn more
03

Braces & Clear Aligners

Straighten crowded, gapped or protruding teeth with metal braces, ceramic braces or near-invisible clear aligners.

Learn more
04

Smile Design & Veneers

A planned combination of veneers, crowns, whitening and reshaping that corrects the colour, shape and proportion of your front teeth.

Learn more
05

Teeth Whitening

Professional bleaching that lifts years of tea, coffee and tobacco staining in a single appointment, without damaging the enamel.

Learn more
06

Crowns & Bridges

Zirconia and ceramic crowns that rebuild a broken or root-treated tooth, and bridges that close a gap without surgery.

Learn more
07

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures, plus implant-supported options for anyone whose current set slips while eating or talking.

Learn more
08

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Planned surgical removal of impacted or painful wisdom teeth, with clear aftercare so healing is straightforward.

Learn more
09

Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

Ultrasonic scaling and polishing that removes the hardened tartar and stain a toothbrush physically cannot shift.

Learn more
10

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

Composite fillings bonded and shaded to match your tooth, so repaired cavities are invisible — no silver, no mercury.

Learn more

Kids Dentistry

Gentle check-ups, fluoride, sealants and milk-tooth treatment — with first visits kept short so children leave without any fear.

Learn more

Gum Disease Treatment

Treatment for bleeding, receding or infected gums — the leading cause of adult tooth loss, and largely reversible when caught early.
